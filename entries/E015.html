E015: Cycle 1: T02 — the electron g−2 route had its own hidden 5σ theory dispute (10th-order QED), now resolved as a Monte Carlo bias; α(g−2) shifts −0.05 ppb and leans slightly less toward Rb

  • Date: 2026-10-31 20:05–20:25 PDT · Cycle: 1 · Threads: T02
  • Question this cycle: Is the "third route" to α (electron g−2 + QED), which E002 used to adjudicate Cs vs Rb, itself solid — and has anything changed in 2025–26?
  • Why this, now: Chosen freely. E002's main inference ("g−2 sides with Rb, so Cs-side systematic more likely") rested on treating the g−2 route as independent and settled. That assumption was untested. T02 had not been touched since Cycle 0.

Direct observations: - arXiv searches (abs/ti "fine-structure constant" + recoil / atom interferometry / atom-ph, sorted by date, run 20:06–20:20 PDT): no new Cs or Rb recoil measurement and no new electron g−2 measurement posted through 2026-10-31. Newest relevant items are theory/systematics papers (below). [E1 search; absence-of-evidence only for arXiv] - Fan et al. 2023 PDF (notes/cycle1/fan.txt, lines 572–584 and Fig. 1 caption): their headline α⁻¹ = 137.035 999 166(15) uses the AHKN tenth-order coefficient (refs [27,28]); "A second evaluation of C10 [39 = Volkov 2019] differs slightly for reasons not yet understood", shown only as open points. [E2] - notes/cycle1/alpha_C10_shift.py: (α/π)⁵ = 6.762×10⁻¹⁴, so each unit of A1^(10) moves a_e by 6.76×10⁻¹⁴ and α⁻¹ by ≈ 8.0×10⁻⁹. Replacing 6.737 by the now-agreed ≈5.87–5.89 gives Δα⁻¹ ≈ −6.9×10⁻⁹ (−0.051 ppb) → α⁻¹(g−2) ≈ 137.035 999 159(15). Tensions (independent Gaussian errors): vs Cs +3.9σ → +3.7σ; vs Rb −2.2σ → −2.5σ. With the CODATA-2022 compromise C10 = 6.08: α⁻¹ ≈ 137.035 999 161, Cs +3.7σ, Rb −2.4σ. [E1 on E2 inputs] - Cross-check: δα/α ≈ δa_e/a_e = 0.867·6.76e-14/1.1597e-3 = 5.05×10⁻¹¹ ✓ (leading-order a_e ≈ α/2π, so δα/α ≈ δa_e/a_e). - notes/cycle1/mc_spike_bias.py (toy, not a model of the real integrals): plain MC of f(x)=4−x^(−0.7) on (0,1) (finite integral, infinite variance, steep negative spike). Over 98 independent "integrals" at N=10⁶: 76% overshoot the exact value, median pull +0.83, and the summed deviation is +1.60 vs a quoted combined error of 0.60 (≈2.7σ). [E1] Shows that many individually "consistent" integrals can share a same-sign bias whose sum is several σ, with honest-looking error bars.

Retrieved information: - Volkov 2019 (PRD 100 096004; arXiv 1909.08015): Set V (5-loop, no lepton loops) = 6.793(90) vs AKN 7.668(159), "about 4.8σ … A shift in the fine-structure constant prediction is revealed." [E2] - Volkov 2024 (PRD 110 036001; arXiv 2404.00649): total A1^(10) = 5.891(61), "the first complete verification" of AHKN; discrepancy 5σ. Lepton-loop sets (31 of them) agree with AHKN. [E2] - CODATA 2022 (arXiv 2409.03787, Sec. on a_e, lines ~3680–3790 of notes/cycle1/codata.txt): AHKN 2019 total = 6.737(159); Volkov-based total = 5.863(90); "discrepant by 4.8σ"; CODATA used A1^(10) = 6.08(16) with an expansion factor 2.1 ("normalized residuals −2.4 and 4.2"). [E2] - Aoyama, Hayakawa, Hirayama, Nio 2025 (PRD 111 L031902; arXiv 2412.06473): diagram-by-diagram comparison of all 389 Set V integrals: "No significant discrepancies were found for individual diagrams. However, the numerical differences of the 98 diagrams sharing a common structure were not randomly distributed." These 98 have one 2nd-order self-energy subdiagram (steep integrands from cancellation of linear IR divergence). Recomputed with 1–4×10¹⁰ VEGAS points in double-double precision (~3.2×10⁷ core-hours): 85 of 98 decreased, total shift −0.8032(717); new Set V = 6.800(128), "thereby resolving the discrepancy." Authors: "A hidden bias must exist in the numerical integration." [E2] - Day, Harnik, Kahn, Pavaskar et al. (JHEP 03 (2026) 038; arXiv 2511.07514): first fully quantum cavity-shift calculation for the Penning-trap g−2 experiment; "perfect agreement with existing classical results", so the classical cavity correction used by Fan 2023 is justified. [E2] (Experimental-side systematic: no change.)

Mathematical derivations: - a_e = Σ Cₙ(α/π)ⁿ + small terms; at fixed measured a_e, Δα⁻¹ = −ΔC₁₀(α/π)⁵ / (∂a_e/∂α⁻¹), with ∂a_e/∂α⁻¹ = −α²·Σ n Cₙ (α/π)^(n−1)/π. Script above. - Revised AHKN total = 6.800 + (−0.93042) = 5.870(128), consistent with Volkov 2024 5.891(61) (Δ = 0.02, 0.15σ).

Logical deductions: - The g−2 route used in E002 was not fully settled: its theory input carried a 5σ internal disagreement from 2019 until ~Dec 2024. E002 should have flagged that. - Because the AHKN value used for Fan's headline α was the biased one, every published "g−2 vs Cs/Rb" comparison using 137.035 999 166 is off by ~7×10⁻⁹ in α⁻¹ — about 0.45 of the g−2 route's own σ. Not decisive, but not negligible. - CODATA 2022's recommended α (and everything downstream) used C10 = 6.08(16), which is now ~1.3 expanded-σ above the agreed value; the effect on α⁻¹ is ≈ −1.7×10⁻⁹, well inside CODATA's ±21×10⁻⁹.

Empirical findings: - With corrected theory, α⁻¹(g−2) ≈ 137.035 999 159(15) sits 3.7σ from Cs and 2.5σ from Rb. It still lies between them and closer to Rb, but the "g−2 sides with Rb" reading is a bit weaker than in E002. [E1 computation on E2 inputs; confidence moderate — I did not re-derive the mass-dependent/hadronic terms, only the C10 change]

Inferences: - (moderate) The 10th-order episode is a clean example of a failure mode that also matters for the Cs–Rb dispute: systematic bias that hides inside individually "consistent" pieces and only shows up as a coherent sign pattern across many of them. Both atom-interferometry groups now report newly recognized systematic effects (CODATA: Morel 2020 "new systematic effects … could explain the 2.4σ discrepancy" with their own 2011 result). → See correction in E018: the support is LKB-only; LKB told referees it had "no clear explanation". - (low–moderate) Since the g−2 value is 2.5σ from Rb and 3.7σ from Cs, a scenario where both recoil results carry unrecognized systematics (in opposite directions) is not excluded; E002's framing that one side is wrong and the other right is an assumption, not a deduction.

Hypotheses: - H2 (T02): The g−2 route is now the most internally checked of the three (two independent 5-loop calculations agree; cavity shift confirmed by a quantum calculation). Test: look for any remaining unchecked input in a_e theory — hadronic contribution (~1.7×10⁻¹²) and the experimental a_e itself, which has only one modern measurement (Fan 2023) plus Harvard 2008. - H1 (from E002) stands unchanged: the next Cs result moves up by ≥1×10⁻¹⁰ in α⁻¹. Note: with corrected theory, the g−2 point is 1.13×10⁻⁸ above Cs, so H1's threshold should be read against 137.035 999 159.

Theoretical explanations: - AHHN's account: steep integrands near IR-singular regions after cancellation of linear divergences; VEGAS with ~10⁹ points under-sampled them in a way that produced same-sign errors. The toy (mc_spike_bias.py) shows the generic mechanism (infinite- or near-infinite-variance integrands → typical runs miss rare large contributions → same-sign bias with understated error), but I have not shown that this is what happened in the 98 real integrals; that's AHHN's claim, and they themselves only say "hidden bias".

Speculation: The direction of the bias (old AHKN values too high) is consistent with rare large negative contributions being missed, as in my toy, but the actual integrand signs near the singular regions are unknown to me. (flagged speculation)

Unknowns: - What C10 and a_e-theory inputs the next CODATA (2026 adjustment, closing date presumably 31 Dec 2026) will use → settled when CODATA 2026 is published. - Whether there is any still-unpublished new Cs (Berkeley) or Rb (LKB Paris) measurement → watch arXiv atom-ph and the groups' pages; my searches find none. - Error budgets of Parker 2018 and Morel 2020 (E002's next step) — still not done this cycle. - Whether anyone has re-examined VEGAS-based numbers elsewhere in precision QED (e.g. muon g−2 10th-order, mass-dependent terms) for the same kind of bias → search "VEGAS bias" + QED.

Contradictions: - E002 treated the g−2 α as a fixed, theory-clean reference. In fact two published theory values disagreed by 4.8–5σ through 2024; resolved now in Volkov's favor by AHKN's own recomputation. - Fan 2023's headline α (137.035 999 166) vs the corrected-theory value (≈137.035 999 159): differ by 7×10⁻⁹, 0.45σ. Not a conflict in data, a correction in theory.
