  • Why this, now: It turns E001's qualitative impression into a checkable table.

Direct observations: - notes/T01_ledger.csv has 20 rows: 15 AI claims, plus 2 competitions, 1 deflated claim, and 2 human baselines. - Pandas count over the 15 AI claims by quantifier: ∃ = 10, ∀ = 2 (L12 Erdős #728, L14 eigenweights), mixed/narrow = 2, n/a = 1. The new-definition column shows 0 confirmed, 1 candidate (L14), and 1 rediscovery (L18).

Retrieved information: - Feng et al. (arXiv:2601.22401, Jan 2026) had Gemini screen 700 Erdős problems labelled "Open." Result: 13 addressed (5 seemingly novel autonomous solutions, 8 already solved in the literature). The authors conclude the problems were open "through obscurity rather than difficulty" and flag the risk of "subconscious plagiarism" [E3, a lab's self-report with human expert evaluation]. - Feng (arXiv:2601.23245) describes an AI agent built on Deep Think. It "employs tools from algebraic combinatorics to connect these eigenweights to the representation theory of symmetric groups, and then determines them for all classical groups." The companion paper (Aletheia, arXiv:2602.10177) calls it "generated by AI without any human intervention" [E3]. - Huang (PNAS 2026, abstract): a public GPT-5.5 Pro with a "brainstorm, execute, review" pipeline produced a correct or essentially correct unit-distance disproof in 8/8 trials, with web search off, at ~107k reasoning tokens per trial. The proofs are "diverse" [E3, abstract only]. - Aggarwal et al. (arXiv:2603.04528): conjecturing and skeptic agents with Lean feedback rediscover the homology / Euler-characteristic relation from incidence-matrix data, using only a linear-algebra vocabulary. Ablations show that symbolic regression alone never finds b₁ or χ [E3]. This is concept rediscovery inside a supplied vocabulary.

Mathematical derivations: — Logical deductions: - The 5/700 novel-solution rate is a lower bound on failures, not a measure of AI capability. It counts only successes on problems selected by obscurity. - If 8/8 public-model trials disprove the unit-distance conjecture with ~10⁵ tokens, then by Gowers's measure the expert hint length needed is small. The PNAS pipeline supplies no problem-specific hints. This is consistent with Gowers's "short hint sequence" hunch, given that the abstract is accurate.

Empirical findings: - (ledger v0, n=13 classifiable AI claims) ∃-type = 10/13 ≈ 77%. That is close to H1's 80% threshold, but the sample is small and selected by what labs chose to announce. Not yet a test of H1: it needs an unbiased sample, e.g. every AI-tagged entry on erdosproblems.com. [E1 count on E2/E3 classifications; low confidence]

Inferences: - (moderate) The ledger's "frontier of conceptual contribution" is currently new connections between existing theories (L14: eigenweights ↔ S_n representation theory; L15: class-field towers ↔ discrete geometry), not new definitions. That is a step above "instances" but short of "concepts." - (low) Ease of reproduction (8/8, cheap) suggests the unit-distance disproof sits in a region of proof space that is reachable by current models without exotic search, which argues against "alien" structure.

Hypotheses: - H2′ (refined): The first AI-originated concept will appear as a new connection that humans then abstract into a definition. Test: track citations of 2601.23245 and 2605.20695 (and their follow-ups) for whether humans define new objects inspired by them (tools/paper_lookup.py cites <openalex-id>). - H4: AI novel-solution rates on "Open" lists are inversely related to how much prior human attention a problem got. Test: correlate the Erdős-database "comments/references count" with AI-solved status.

Theoretical explanations: "Obscurity, not difficulty" + "breadth" explain most successes. Models act as a literature-wide recombination engine. Speculation: If concept formation is mostly naming a recurring pattern across many proofs, the systems that mass-produce proofs (L13-style sweeps) may eventually produce concepts as compression, so the 2603.04528-style setup is the one to watch. Unknowns: - Was L14 independently checked by a human expert other than Feng? → look for a referee report or a Yun/Zhang comment. - The Huang PNAS full text: how "essentially correct" is defined → read the paper and the released outputs. - An unbiased denominator for H1 → scrape erdosproblems.com AI tags. That is read-only and needs care with robots and rate limits.

Contradictions: "Without any human intervention" (Aletheia on L14) vs. a human posing the problem and selecting the agent's tools. This is a definitional issue about what "autonomous" means, recorded rather than resolved.
