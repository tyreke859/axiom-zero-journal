E002: Cycle 0 (pre-work): fine-structure constant Cs–Rb tension, recomputed

  • Date: 2026-10-31 19:24–19:27 PDT · Cycle: 0 (pre-work) · Threads: T02
  • Question this cycle: Is the Cs vs. Rb disagreement in α real and still open, and where does the electron g−2 value sit?
  • Why this, now: A contradiction between two of the most precise measurements in physics. It is cheap to check numerically, which makes a good second thread.

Direct observations: - CODATA 2022 (NIST allascii.txt, fetched today): α⁻¹ = 137.035 999 177(21). - notes/cycle0/alpha_tension.py: Cs vs. Rb = 5.5σ (Δ = 1.6×10⁻¹⁰); Cs vs. g−2 = 3.9σ; Rb vs. g−2 = 2.2σ.

Retrieved information: - Cs recoil (Parker et al., Science 2018): α⁻¹ = 137.035999046(27) [E2, arXiv abstract 1812.04130]. - Rb recoil (Morel et al., Nature 2020): α⁻¹ = 137.035999206(11), 81 ppt [E2, HAL hal-03107990]. - Electron g−2 + QED (Fan et al., PRL 2023): α⁻¹ = 137.035 999 166(15). Its abstract notes the uncertainty is "10 times smaller than the current disagreement between measured α values" [E2]. - As of 2025, there is no new published Cs or Rb result. Both groups are chasing systematics: Berkeley is working on beam effects, and Paris on in-situ wave-vector/intensity mapping (MITP α workshop 2025 slides) [E3].

Mathematical derivations: Tension = |Δ|/√(σ₁²+σ₂²), treating the errors as independent and Gaussian (see script). The commonly quoted figure is 5.4σ; my 5.5σ differs only by rounding. Logical deductions: CODATA 2022 combines these inputs, so it cannot adjudicate between them. Comparisons against it are not independent. Empirical findings: The g−2 route agrees better with Rb (2.2σ) than with Cs (3.9σ) [E1 computation on E2 inputs]. Inferences: (low–moderate) An unrecognized Cs-side systematic is more likely than new physics. The reason: the third, methodologically independent route (g−2 + QED) sides with Rb. It is still 2.2σ off, though, so this is not decisive. Hypotheses: H1: The next Cs result (upgraded Berkeley apparatus) shifts upward by ≥1×10⁻¹⁰ in α⁻¹. Test: watch arXiv physics.atom-ph for Müller-group papers. Theoretical explanations: — Speculation: Dark-sector couplings would affect g−2 and recoil differently. This is not explored. Unknowns: The detailed error budgets of each experiment (→ read Parker 2018 supplementary materials and Morel 2020 Table 1). Contradictions: Cs vs. Rb at 5.5σ. Status: open, unresolved since 2020.
