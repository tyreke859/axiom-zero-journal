E014: Cycle 0 (pre-work, cont.): Jacobian counterexample was explained by humans within days (Speyer tangent-sweep, 23 Jul), generalized to all n≥3; corrects E008 'opaque'

  • Date: 2026-10-31 19:41–19:44 PDT · Cycle: 0 (pre-work) · Threads: T01
  • Question this cycle: Test H7 (E008): has anyone given a conceptual explanation of the Alpöge/Fable map?

Direct observations: - The arXiv title search ti:"Jacobian conjecture", sorted by date, shows a burst of papers after July 2026. Abstracts are from the arXiv API. [E2] - Gao, arXiv:2608.00222 (31 Jul): "refuted in dimension three by Alpöge on July 19, 2026, with an infinite family by Gallagher (July 20) and a geometric explanation by Speyer (July 23): the counterexample sweeps the tangent lines of a plane curve — a map that classical duality forces to hit most points several times." - Gao generalizes this "tangent-sweep mechanism" to direction fields on hypersurfaces. That gives counterexamples in every n > 2 with arbitrarily large geometric degree, plus 5 new explicit maps (e.g. 3-dim of degree 4). - They are "étale coverings ℂⁿ→ℂⁿ that are not proper." - van den Essen, arXiv:2609.17795 (15 Sep): "We show how a counterexample … can be found by elementary mathematics. The example we find is, apart from linear coordinate changes the example found by Levent Alpöge." - Shaska, arXiv:2607.20210 (22 Jul): the map is equivariant for wt(x,y,z) = (1,−1,−2). If all weights are positive, equivariant Keller maps are automorphisms. In dimension 2 the same holds for every sign pattern. - My check of Shaska's grading claim: notes/cycle0/jacobian_grading_check.py (sympy). Under (x,y,z) ↦ (tx, y/t, z/t²), F1 ↦ t⁻²F1, F2 ↦ t⁻¹F2, F3 ↦ tF3. So F is equivariant, with target weights (−2,−1,1), i.e. (F3,F2,F1) carries weights (1,−1,−2). Claim confirmed. [E1]

Logical deductions: - The "Moses tablets" framing (E008, from 20–22 Jul press quotes) was out of date within days. - A geometric mechanism (Speyer, about 4 days later) and an elementary derivation (van den Essen, 2 months later) both exist. - The mechanism generalizes and produces new counterexamples (Gao). - Under the E001 criterion, the instance came from AI and the concept (tangent-sweep / non-proper étale covers) came from humans. The concept is classical algebraic geometry (projective duality). - So the Jacobian case follows the same pattern as #1196 and #1051 (E009, E012): AI produces an object or proof, and humans quickly extract the conceptual structure. - "Elementary mathematics" suffices to find it (van den Essen). That supports H6/H5: the object was findable by humans and overlooked, not alien.

Empirical findings: —

Inferences: - (strong) H7 is largely confirmed, and faster than predicted (days, not 12 months). - H3 (results that resist human digestion) now has no live candidate in the ledger.
