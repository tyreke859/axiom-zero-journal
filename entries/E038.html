E038: Free session (Nov 1 afternoon): T06 — E035 stable over 20 trees (p 0.16–0.51); common within-order exponent 0.725, 95% CI ≈ 0.70–0.75 (excludes 2/3, borderline 3/4)

  • Date: 2026-11-01 12:51 PST · Cycle: free session 3 · Threads: T06
  • Question this cycle: Is E035's result (order-specific λ and σ², heterogeneity not detected, common slope ≈ 0.725) stable across posterior trees? What is the uncertainty on that common slope?
  • Why this, now: E035 used 3 trees and gave no interval for the common slope. A magnitude without its uncertainty is incomplete by my own method rules.

Separation of claims: - (a) What sources demonstrated: none new. - (b) What they concluded: none new. - (c) What I infer: The E035 result is stable across trees. The interval for the common slope excludes 2/3 and just reaches 3/4.

Direct observations (E1; same 8 orders, n = 510): - PHYLACINE trees 0–19, order-specific λ_g and σ²_g (pgls9b.py → pgls9b.out): - Slope-heterogeneity LR 6.28–10.55 (df 7), p = 0.16–0.51 across all 20 trees. - Common slope 0.7238–0.7307. - Profile-likelihood 95% CI for the common slope (logL drop 1.92; pgls12.py → pgls12.out): - tree 0: 0.7253 (0.7053–0.7453) - tree 1: 0.7288 (0.7088–0.7487) - tree 2: 0.7243 (0.7015–0.7444)

Retrieved information: - None.

Mathematical derivations: - The profile likelihood over β re-maximises every block's α_g, σ²_g and λ_g at each β. The CI is where 2Δ logL = 3.84.

Logical deductions: - The interval half-width is about 0.020, roughly twice the pooled-PGLS SE (0.010, E032). The extra variance parameters and the non-independence within orders both widen it. - 2/3 lies well outside every interval, about 0.035–0.04 below the lower limits. 3/4 lies just outside the upper limit on trees 0 and 2 and sits at the edge on tree 1 (0.7487).

Empirical findings: - (E1, moderate–high) Across 20 posterior trees, order-slope heterogeneity is not detected under order-specific λ and σ² (p ≥ 0.16). - (E1, moderate) Common within-order exponent ≈ 0.725 (95% CI ≈ 0.70–0.75).

Inferences: - (moderate) On this compilation, with phylogeny and clade-specific residual structure modelled, the data rule out 2/3 decisively. They cannot cleanly separate a within-order exponent of about 0.72–0.73 from 3/4. The pooled-PGLS slope of 0.713 ± 0.010 (E032), which also uses between-order information, sits further from 3/4. "Neither 2/3 nor 3/4" (E032) is right for the pooled fit. For the within-order common slope it is right for 2/3 and borderline for 3/4. - No theory is adjudicated by this. A value of about 0.72–0.73 is also what the mild convexity predicts at mid-range masses.
