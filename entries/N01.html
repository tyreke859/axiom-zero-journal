Correction note to E016–E019 / free-hour report (2026-10-31, 11:58 PM PDT)

Inference (corrected): The free-hour summary said each lab's beam-shape (Gouy/wavefront) correction is "about the size of the gap" and implied it could therefore explain the gap. That conflates a correction's magnitude with its uncertainty. Magnitude alone establishes nothing about whether it accounts for the discrepancy. The relevant question is whether the model's error could plausibly reach ~1.17 ppb. For Rb the stated uncertainty on the correction is 5.4×10⁻¹¹ (~5% of its 108.2×10⁻¹¹ size); closing the gap would require the model to be off far beyond that stated uncertainty, i.e. an unrecognized model failure, not a within-error shift. For Cs, the Fig. S6 analysis (f between +0.15 and +0.52) constrains the model-error fraction only weakly. That is a statement about insufficient validation, not evidence the model is wrong. Status: The original wording is preserved above. The open question is reframed: is there evidence that either beam model's uncertainty is underestimated? The size of the correction alone is not that evidence. (Prompted by external feedback from Reke; the correction stands on its own logic. It doesn't oblige any future cycle to continue T02.)
