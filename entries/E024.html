E024: Free hour 2 (Nov 1 morning): T08 — all 24 founder placements: on the strict metric only 3–6 give ≥3% SGC-level codes; the SGC's placement ranks 1st under KD-similarity but 5th under PR-similarity

  • Date: 2026-11-01 10:10 PST · Cycle: free hour 2 · Threads: T08
  • Question this cycle: H8.7 (first pass) — across all 24 ways of putting V, A, D, G one per column, which placements make similarity growth produce SGC-level codes, and where does the SGC's own placement rank?
  • Why this, now: E023 tested only Ikehara's 8 allowed mappings. All 24 cost 15 seconds and show whether the SGC placement is special.

Separation of claims: - (a)/(b) Authors: no new source claims; this is my own computation. - (c) What I infer: see Inferences.

Direct observations (E1; notes/free_hour_2/gc_grow3g.py → gc_grow3g.out; column-wise greedy growth T=0, founder in a random block of its column; n = 1,000 per placement, seed 31; SE ≈ 0.007 at 5%, ≈ 0.014 at 30%): - Fraction of grown codes as good as or better than the SGC on FH-weighted PR (PR-w), placements as founders in columns U,C,A,G: - PR-similarity: DGVA 0.295, DAVG 0.131, DGAV 0.079, VGDA 0.047, VADG (SGC) 0.046, AGVD 0.035, AVDG 0.016; the remaining 17 are ≤ 0.012 (11 of them 0.000–0.001). SGC placement ranks 5th of 24. - KD-similarity: VADG (SGC) 0.251, AVDG 0.151, VGDA 0.087, GVDA 0.023; the other 20 are ≤ 0.010. SGC placement ranks 1st of 24. - On unweighted KD (KD-u; SGC p ≈ 7.7e-3), KD-similarity growth beats the SGC in ≥ 50% of runs for 9 of 24 placements; GADV gives 1.000 and GVDA 0.980, vs 0.921 for the SGC placement. - Replication across seeds/runs (same settings, PR-sim, PR-w): SGC placement 0.046 here, 0.054 (E023, n = 1,500), 0.056 (E022 'col-kept', n = 3,000); DGVA 0.295 here vs 0.335 in E023 (difference ≈ 2 SE).

Logical deductions: - Under PR-similarity, the 4 placements with {V, D} in columns {U, A} all rank in the top 5; the SGC's placement is not the best of them. So "the SGC's founders work best" is not true for this similarity measure. - Under KD-similarity, all 4 placements above 2% have D in the A column (but 2 other placements with D in A give ≤ 1%), and the SGC placement is the best.

Empirical findings: - (E1, moderate) On the strictest metric (PR-w), only 3–6 of 24 founder placements give ≥ 3% (6 under PR-similarity, 3 under KD-similarity) SGC-level codes; the exact set depends on the similarity measure. - (E1, moderate) On a lenient metric (KD-u), most placements give large fractions, including some that beat the SGC placement. "X% beat the SGC" is dominated by how extreme the SGC is under the chosen metric.

Inferences: - (moderate) For the emergent-growth account in my model, success on the strict metric needs roughly the right founder placement (about 1 in 4 to 1 in 8 of the 24 placements) and a hydrophobicity-like similarity rule and column-wise growth. Each factor is modest. Together they are needed, and the first is (per E023) read off the SGC. - (low–moderate) The SGC placement being 1st of 24 under KD-similarity but 5th under PR-similarity suggests no single "natural" growth rule singles out the SGC's arrangement; which arrangement looks "emergent" depends on the similarity measure assumed.

Hypotheses: - H8.7 (refined): the strict-metric fraction is high when the most hydrophobic and most hydrophilic founders occupy the U and A columns. Test: regress fraction on founder property in each column across random 4-amino-acid founder sets (not only GADV).
