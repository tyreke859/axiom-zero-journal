E027: Free hour 2 (Nov 1 morning): T08 / H8.1 under KD — robustness still spread over 18/20 blocks, but the worse-than-chance blocks change (R, C instead of D, Y)

  • Date: 2026-11-01 10:14 PST · Cycle: free hour 2 · Threads: T08
  • Question this cycle: Does E026's block picture (robustness spread over most blocks; Asp and Tyr the only worse-than-chance blocks) hold under the Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy scale (KD)?
  • Why this, now: E026 listed "not repeated with KD" as an unknown, and E026's inference tied D/Y to the A-column story of E025. If the "bad" blocks change with the scale, that inference is scale-specific.

Separation of claims: - (a)/(b) Authors: no new source claims; own computation. - (c) What I infer: see Inferences.

Direct observations (E1; notes/free_hour_2/gc_block_kd.py KD → gc_block_kd.out (identical to gc_block.py except the property); pair_costs.py → pair_costs.out; same seed, 1M permutations, same definitions as E026): - Whole code: KD unweighted p = 7.7e-3 (z = 2.30); FH-weighted p = 8.8e-5 (z = 3.68). Both match the E020/E022 baselines (7.7e-3, 8.8e-5). - FH-weighted block contribution relative to the null: 18 of 20 below 1. The lowest are K 0.093, Q 0.098, H 0.118, F 0.162, N 0.163. Above 1: R 1.011, C 1.187. Unweighted: above 1 are L 1.150, I 1.186, R 1.271. - Under KD, D and Y are below 1 (weighted 0.191 and 0.361). - Costliest pair (weighted KD): R–C (CGY↔UGY, a first-position change; KD −4.5 vs 2.5) = 0.294 = 6.4% of MS. Under PR (re-checked in the same script) the costliest pair is D–Y = 6.6%, as in E026. - Leave-one-block-out, weighted KD: z ranges 3.10–3.99, compared with 3.68 for the full code. The highest is from removing R (3.99, p 1e-5).

Retrieved information: - None new.

Logical deductions: - The "spread over 18/20 blocks" result holds under both scales. - Which two blocks are worse than chance depends on the scale (PR: D, Y; KD: R, C). So the blocks' identities are not a scale-free property of the code.

Empirical findings: - (E1, high within this model) Under both PR and KD with FH98 weights, the SGC's robustness is not concentrated in a few blocks. Removing any one block changes the weighted z by at most about 0.7. - (E1) In both scales the single costliest pair is a first-position U↔C (R–C: CGY↔UGY) or U↔G (D–Y: UAY↔GAY) change between pyrimidine-ending codons with the same second base.

Inferences (E3, labelled): - E026's inference linked the D/Y exception to the A-column story. That link is PR-specific. Under KD, the A column (D E H K N Q Y) contains 5 of the 7 lowest-ratio blocks (K, Q, H, N, E), so it looks more uniformly well-placed. That fits E025's finding that a polar A column drives success, and the evidence for it is stronger under KD than under PR.

Unknowns / not checked: - I have not checked whether the literature singles out Arg (or the D–Y boundary) as a known outlier. That needs a source check, not done. - Same limitations as E026: block space fixed, FH98 weights not independently derived.

Corrections to earlier entries: - Refinement of E026 (2026-11-01): E026's labelled inference about D/Y and the A column holds under PR only. Under KD, the worse-than-chance blocks are R and C, and the A column is among the best-placed (E027).
