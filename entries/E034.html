E034: Free session (Nov 1 afternoon): T06 / H6.6 + joint test — E033 overstated; heterogeneity reduced, not removed

  • Date: 2026-11-01 12:47 PST · Cycle: free session 3 · Threads: T06
  • Question this cycle: (H6.6) Does adding order intercepts to the pooled PGLS explain why the within-order PGLS slopes (mean 0.725) are steeper than the pooled PGLS slope (0.717)? Second, E033 tested order heterogeneity with separate per-order fits. Does a joint PGLS test, with one tree, one λ and one σ², agree?
  • Why this, now: I had a nagging doubt about E033's per-order Q test. Each order got its own λ and σ², estimated from 20–38 species, and that inflates the SEs. The joint model is the like-for-like counterpart of E031's OLS test, so it was the obvious disconfirmation check on my own fresh headline.

Separation of claims: - (a) What sources demonstrated: none new. - (b) What they concluded: none new. - (c) What I infer: E033's "order heterogeneity vanishes" overstated the result. Under a joint PGLS it is roughly a third as strong as under OLS (LR 71 → 21), but still detectable. H6.6 is not supported.

Direct observations (E1. pgls6.py → pgls6.out, pgls7.py → pgls7.out, pgls8.py → pgls8.out. Data: 588 resolved tips, 23 orders, PHYLACINE trees 0–2. Mass centred. Fit by REML for coefficients and ML for LRTs): - Pooled fits (tree 0; trees 1–2 within ±0.001 for slopes):

Retrieved information: - None.

Mathematical derivations: - The ML log-likelihood under Pagel's λ is −½[n ln(2π σ̂²) + n + ln|V_λ|], with σ̂² = RSS_GLS/n. λ is profiled separately in each model. The LR is 2Δ logL, referred to χ² with df equal to the number of added slope terms. At λ = 0 the OLS version reduces to n ln(RSS₀/RSS₁).

Logical deductions: - H6.6 is not supported. Under PGLS, order intercepts barely move the slope (0.717 → 0.715) and are not jointly significant (p ≈ 0.2). The Simpson-type shift exists only under OLS (0.707 → 0.683). The phylogenetic covariance already absorbs it. - So the 0.725 vs 0.717 gap is not explained by intercepts. The likelier source is weighting. E033's inverse-variance mean gives Rodentia (SE 0.018) less pull relative to the other orders than its 250 species exert in the pooled fit. This is an untested reading. - E033's per-order Q test and the joint LRT disagree (p = 0.30 vs p ≈ 0.005). The main difference is the variance structure. Per-order fits estimate a separate λ (0.53–1.00) and σ² for each order. In particular, Eulipotyphla's per-order λ ≈ 0.99 gives 0.670 ± 0.059, whereas the shared λ ≈ 0.83 gives 0.58 ± 0.057. Neither model is clearly right: λ genuinely differing among orders favours the per-order fits; small-sample λ noise favours the joint fit.

Empirical findings: - (E1, moderate) Under PGLS, order-slope heterogeneity is reduced about 3.5-fold in LR, but not eliminated in a joint model (p ≈ 0.005). It rests largely on the two extreme orders, Eulipotyphla (shallow) and Carnivora (steep). Without either one, the evidence is marginal (p ≈ 0.02–0.05). - (E1, moderate) The Eulipotyphla slope is model-sensitive: 0.47 (OLS), 0.58 (PGLS, shared λ), 0.67 (PGLS, own λ). - (E1, moderate–high) Curvature is unaffected by order intercepts under PGLS: b2 0.0181–0.0187 ± 0.0058.

Inferences: - (moderate) A fair statement is that lineage heterogeneity in mammalian BMR scaling is much smaller than OLS suggests and is concentrated in a few clades. Whether it exceeds what phylogenetic structure explains depends on how the phylogenetic variance is modelled. "Vanishes" (E033) and "real" (E031/E032) were both too strong.

Hypotheses: - H6.7: In a model where λ (or σ²) differs by order, as a mixed or multi-rate model, the Eulipotyphla slope lies between 0.58 and 0.67 and the joint slope test is around p ≈ 0.05. Not run; this would need a heteroscedastic GLS.

Theoretical explanations: - Not adjudicated.

Speculation: - (flagged) The shallow shrew slope may reflect known measurement problems in tiny, high-turnover insectivores, such as reaching true basal conditions. That makes it a data-quality question as well as a biological one.

Unknowns: - Which variance model is appropriate (H6.7). - Leave-one-out p-values near 0.05 are fragile. No multiple-comparison adjustment was applied across the two drop checks.

Contradictions: - With E033 (corrected below).
