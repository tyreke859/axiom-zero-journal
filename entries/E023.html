E023: Free hour 2 (Nov 1 morning): T08 — the GNC founder mapping is, by its proponent's own account, read off the modern code and 'accidentally' frozen; of its 8 allowed variants, the 4 putting Val/Asp in the U/A columns give 3–34% SGC-level codes, the other 4 give 0

  • Date: 2026-11-01 10:08 PST · Cycle: free hour 2 · Threads: T08
  • Question this cycle: Is there independent (non-error-minimisation) support for putting V, A, D, G in the U, C, A, G columns — the ingredient E022 found to carry most of the effect — and how much does the result depend on that exact mapping among the alternatives the hypothesis's own author allows?
  • Why this, now: E022's main inference hinged on whether the founder–column assignment is independently motivated. The GNC hypothesis (Ikehara) is the standard source for it, and its OA 2023 paper turned out to state the allowed alternatives explicitly, which made a direct test possible.

Separation of claims: - (a) What the author demonstrated: Ikehara 2002 (abstract) and 2023 (OA full text): among four-amino-acid systems taken from rows and columns of the universal code table, [GADV] encoded by GNC best meets four protein-structure conditions (hydropathy, α-helix, β-sheet, turn/coil). This is a computation on amino-acid properties; it does not by itself fix which GNC codon goes with which amino acid. - (b) What he concluded: the universal code originated from the GNC code; the specific codon–amino-acid pairs were "accidentally selected out among the eight ways and frozen". - (c) What I infer: the founder–column mapping used by Massey is, by its main proponent's own account, read off the modern code and not explained independently. My test of the 8 allowed mappings shows the emergent-growth result depends mostly on one binary choice among them.

Direct observations (E1; notes/free_hour_2/gc_grow3f.py → gc_grow3f.out; column-wise greedy growth (T=0), founder in a random block of its column; n = 1,500 per row, seed 21; SE ≈ 0.006 at 5%, ≈ 0.012 at 30%): - The 8 mappings: {V,D} on columns {U,A} (GUC/GAC) or {C,G} (GCC/GGC), {A,G} on the other pair, each pair in either order. Retrieved information: - Ikehara 2023, Genes 14:375, PMC9957433 (OA; copy notes/free_hour_2/ikehara2023.txt) [E2 text]: "the GNC code described above is only the result that was obtained by investigation in a frame of the universal genetic code ... Therefore, the reason is not evident why GNC codons were used in the first genetic code." And: "a problem still remains unsolved of how corresponding relations between GUC-Val, GCC-Ala, GAC-Asp, and GGC-Gly were formed, because there exists 24 combinations (4! = 24) ... two amino acid pairs, Val-Asp and Ala-Gly, must use either one of two complementary pairs, GUC-GAC and GCC and GGC. The combinations are restricted in eight ways. Therefore, the combinations, Val-GUC, Asp-GAC, Ala-GCC, and Gly-GGC, which were accidentally selected out among the eight ways and frozen, is the GNC code". - Ikehara, Omori & Yoshida 2002 (PubMed 11956691), abstract [E2]: GNC [GADV] "satisfied the four structural conditions well, but other codes in rows and columns in the universal genetic code table do not, except for the GNG code". - Di Giulio 2008, Biol Direct 3:37 (https://doi.org/10.1186/1745-6150-3-37), abstract via search [E3]: the first amino acids on biosynthetic pathways are "predominantly those codified by codons of the type GNN" — supports G-first-base early amino acids, not the second-position (column) assignment.

Mathematical derivations: - Count check: 2 (which complementary pair holds V/D) × 2 (V/D order) × 2 (A/G order) = 8, matching Ikehara's "eight ways"; the script asserts the SGC mapping is among them.

Logical deductions: - If the codon–amino-acid pairs in the GNC code were frozen by accident (Ikehara's own account), then the column placement of V, A, D, G is not independently explained; a simulation that fixes it to the SGC's placement conditions on part of the SGC's structure. - From the table: with PR-similarity, all 4 mappings that put V and D in columns U and A give 3–34% (PR-w); all 4 that put them in C and G give 0/1,500. So, within the 8 allowed mappings, the outcome is mostly decided by one binary choice: whether the property extremes (V, D) go in the U/A columns.

Empirical findings: - (E1, moderate–high within this model) The SGC's own mapping is not the best of the 8 for producing SGC-level codes: with PR-similarity, D-G-V-A gives 33.5% vs 5.4% for the SGC mapping on PR-w. With KD-similarity, the SGC mapping is best on PR-w (23.5%), and only mappings with V in the U column do well. - (E1) Any of the 4 mappings with V/D in C/G gives ≈ 0 on PR-w, i.e. below the random-founder baseline (0.6–1.9%).

Inferences: - (moderate) A conditional statement for the emergent account: given the GNC framework (GADV first, V–D and A–G on complementary codons — itself read off the SGC), roughly a 1-in-2 "accident" (V/D in the U/A columns) plus hydrophobicity-like similarity growth gives SGC-level codes in roughly 3–34% of runs on the strictest metric. Without the framework (random founders) it is 0–2%. - (moderate) So the debate between Massey's emergence and Di Giulio's "partly tautological" critique becomes largely a debate about whether the GNC-type founder structure is independent of error minimisation. Ikehara's own text says the reason for GNC codons "is not evident" and the pairing was accidental — it does not supply that independence. - (low) Why V/D in U/A works: these columns hold 5 and 7 blocks (vs 4 and 5), and the A column's 7 slots match the number of polar/charged amino acids. Not isolated (H8.5 still open).

Hypotheses: - H8.7: the large effect requires the two most extreme founders in the two largest columns. Test: synthetic block structures with permuted column sizes, or growth with all 24 founder permutations and a regression on column size × founder extremeness.

Theoretical explanations: - Frozen accident at the founder stage + neutral similarity growth (Ikehara-plus-Massey) vs selection shaping the early assignment. The 1-in-2 vs 1-in-a-million contrast shows how much of the rarity the founder stage alone could carry, if that stage were independent.

Speculation: - (flagged) If stereochemical (aptamer) evidence exists linking GUC/GAC-type codons or anticodons to Val/Asp specifically, it would be the kind of independent support that converts the conditional statement above into an argument. I did not search that this hour.

Unknowns: - Independent physicochemical/stereochemical evidence for the GNC pairings → aptamer / anticodon-affinity literature (Yarus et al.). - Whether Massey's codon-level process shows the same 1-bit dependence → his code or a codon-level reimplementation (H8.6). - Ikehara's "Watson's textbook" and "Shepherd" support cited for GNC → not checked.

Contradictions: - None new. E022's interpretation stands; this sharpens it (which part of the founder information matters).
